# Shipping Document Examples

## Overview

This guide explains the shipping documents used in ECTN certificate applications.

Each section explains:
- What the document is
- Where to obtain the document
- An example of the document.

The required documents depend on the country of the consignee and the type of shipment.

Refer to the [Country Guides](../../country-guides/) for country-specific requirements.

## Shipping Documents

### Bill of Lading (BL)

**What is it?**

A bill of lading is a shipping document issued by the shipping line or an authorized issuing party.

It identifies the shipper, consignee, cargo, vessel, and shipping route.

**Where to obtain it:** Request the bill of lading from your shipping line or freight forwarder.

**Document examples:**
- [Maersk Bill of Lading](bill-of-lading/maersk.pdf)
- [MSC Bill of Lading](bill-of-lading/msc.pdf)
- [CMA CGM Bill of Lading](bill-of-lading/cma-cgm.pdf)
- [Hapag-Lloyd Bill of Lading](bill-of-lading/hapag-lloyd.pdf)
- [Grimaldi Bill of Lading](bill-of-lading/grimaldi.pdf)
- [UAL Bill of Lading](bill-of-lading/ual.pdf)

### Commercial Invoice

**What is it?**

A commercial invoice gives the details of the sale of goods.

It identifies the seller, buyer, goods description, and commercial value.

**Where to obtain it:** Request the commercial invoice from the seller or exporter of the goods.

**Document example:** [Commercial Invoice](commercial-invoice.pdf)

### Freight Invoice

**What is it?**

A freight invoice gives the transportation charges for a shipment.

It identifies the freight cost charged by the shipping line or freight forwarder.

**Where to obtain it:** Request the freight invoice from the shipping line or freight forwarder that arranged the shipment.

**Document example:** [Freight Invoice](freight-invoice.pdf)

### Packing List

**What is it?**

A packing list gives information about the goods and their packaging.

It usually identifies the number of packages, goods description, weights, and dimensions.

**Where to obtain it:** Request the packing list from the shipper or exporter.

**Document example:** [Packing List](packing-list.pdf)

### Export Declaration

**What is it?**

An export declaration is a customs document for goods exported from a country.

It gives information about the goods and their export.

**Where to obtain it:** Request the export declaration from the exporter or the customs broker that completed the export procedure.

**Document example:** [Export Declaration](export-declaration.pdf)

### Certificate of Origin

**What is it?**

A certificate of origin identifies the country of origin of the goods.

**Where to obtain it:** Request the certificate from the exporter or the organization authorized to issue it in the country of origin.

**Document example:** [Certificate of Origin](certificate-of-origin.pdf)

### Certificate of Insurance

**What is it?**

A certificate of insurance gives information about insurance coverage for the shipment.

**Where to obtain it:** Request the certificate from the insurance provider or the party that arranged the cargo insurance.

**Document example:** [Certificate of Insurance](certificate-of-insurance.pdf)

## Country-Specific Documents

Some countries require additional documents for ECTN applications.

### Documento Único (DU) — Angola

**What is it?**

The Documento Único (DU) is an Angolan import document.

**Where to obtain it:** Request the DU from the consignee or the customs broker in Angola.

**Document example:** [Documento Único](documento-unico.pdf)

### ARCCLA Form — Angola

**What is it?**

The ARCCLA form is a document required for an Angola ARCCLA certificate application.

The form must be signed and stamped.

**Where to obtain it:** Download the form template from AfricaCTN.

**Document template:** [ARCCLA Form](arccla-form.xlsx)

### FDI Import License

**What is it?**

The FDI import license is an import document. The applicable country guide determines when this document is required.

**Where to obtain it:** Contact the consignee or the customs broker responsible for the import procedure. Confirm the issuing authority for the applicable shipment.

**Document example:** [FDI Import License](fdi-import-license.pdf)

### GUCE Import License

**What is it?**

The GUCE import license is an import document. The applicable country guide determines when this document is required.

**Where to obtain it:** Contact the consignee or the customs broker responsible for the import procedure. Confirm the issuing authority for the applicable shipment.

**Document example:** [GUCE Import License](guce-import-license.pdf)

## Important Information

The documents in this folder are reference examples.

A document example does not confirm that the same document is required for every country.

Document requirements can differ by country, shipment type, and application stage.

Refer to the applicable country guide before you send documents to AfricaCTN.
