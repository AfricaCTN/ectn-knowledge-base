# ECTN and Shipping Terminology Glossary

## Overview

This glossary explains common shipping terms used in ECTN certificate applications.

Use this glossary to understand the terms in shipping documents and country guides.

For certificate names and application procedures, refer to [general.md](general.md).

For country-specific requirements, refer to the [Country Guides](country-guides/).

## Shipping Parties

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Shipper** | The person or company that sends the cargo. The bill of lading identifies the shipper. |
| **Consignee** | The person or company identified as the consignee on the bill of lading. |
| **Notify Party** | The person or company that the shipping line must notify about the cargo's arrival. |
| **Freight Forwarder** | A company that arranges cargo transportation for a customer. |
| **Shipping Line** | A company that operates or arranges maritime cargo transportation. |
| **Port Agent** | A person or company that represents a shipping line or vessel at a port. |

## Shipping Locations and Routes

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Port of Loading (POL)** | The port where the cargo is loaded onto a vessel. |
| **Port of Discharge (POD)** | The port where the cargo is discharged from a vessel. |
| **Final Destination** | The country or location where the cargo is delivered after transportation. |
| **Consignee Country** | The country where the consignee is located. The routing tables use this country as the final destination. |
| **Transit Cargo** | Cargo that passes through one country before it reaches its final destination in another country. |
| **Transshipment** | The transfer of cargo from one vessel to another vessel during transportation. |

> **Note:** The port of discharge and consignee country can be different. Some shipments require certificates for both countries.

## Shipping Documents

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Bill of Lading (BL)** | A shipping document that identifies the cargo, shipper, consignee, and transportation details. |
| **Master Bill of Lading (MBL)** | A bill of lading issued by a shipping line to the party that books the shipment. |
| **House Bill of Lading (HBL)** | A bill of lading issued by a freight forwarder or non-vessel-operating common carrier. |
| **Commercial Invoice** | A document that gives the goods description, commercial value, and transaction details. |
| **Proforma Invoice** | A preliminary invoice that gives the proposed goods and transaction details. |
| **Freight Invoice** | A document that gives the freight charges for the transportation of cargo. |
| **Packing List** | A document that gives the cargo quantities, packages, weights, and packing details. |
| **Certificate of Origin** | A document that identifies the country where the goods originate. |
| **Export Declaration** | A customs document that gives information about goods exported from a country. |
| **Import License** | An authorization that permits the import of specified goods into a country. |
| **Insurance Certificate** | A document that gives information about insurance coverage for the cargo. |

## Vessel and Shipment Information

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Vessel** | A ship that transports cargo by sea. |
| **Voyage Number** | A reference that identifies a vessel's voyage. |
| **Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA)** | The estimated date and time when a vessel will arrive at a port. |
| **Estimated Time of Departure (ETD)** | The estimated date and time when a vessel will depart from a port. |
| **Shipped on Board Date** | The date that confirms when the cargo was loaded onto the vessel. |
| **Container Number** | A unique identification number assigned to a shipping container. |
| **Seal Number** | A reference number that identifies the seal installed on a container. |
| **HS Code** | A Harmonized System code used to classify goods for customs purposes. |
| **Incoterm** | A trade term that defines responsibilities for transportation, costs, and risks between the buyer and seller. |

## ECTN Application Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Draft Certificate** | A preliminary ECTN certificate prepared for customer review. |
| **Draft Approval** | The customer's confirmation that the information on the draft certificate is correct. |
| **Certificate Reference Number** | A reference that identifies an ECTN application or certificate. The type of reference depends on the country and application stage. |
| **Validation** | The process in which the relevant authorities examine and approve an ECTN certificate. |
| **Validated Certificate** | An ECTN certificate that the relevant authorities approved. |
| **Validation Deadline** | The final date or time by which the relevant authorities must validate the certificate. |
| **Regularization** | A procedure that addresses an ECTN application that does not meet the normal requirements or deadline. The applicable rules depend on the country. |
| **Correction** | A change to information on an ECTN certificate. The applicable rules depend on the country and certificate status. |

> **Note:** A draft approval or draft reference number does not confirm that the authorities validated the certificate.

## Cargo and Measurement Terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Full Container Load (FCL)** | Cargo transported in a container allocated to one shipment. |
| **Less than Container Load (LCL)** | Cargo transported in a container with cargo from other shipments. |
| **Bulk Cargo** | Cargo transported in large quantities without individual packaging. |
| **General Cargo** | Cargo transported as individual packages, pallets, or other separate units. |
| **Roll-on/Roll-off (Roro)** | Cargo that moves onto or off a vessel on wheels. |
| **Gross Weight** | The total weight of the goods and their packaging, as stated on the shipping document. |
| **Net Weight** | The weight of the goods without their packaging. |
| **Cubic Meter (CBM)** | A unit of volume equal to one cubic meter. |
| **Metric Ton (MT)** | A unit of weight equal to 1,000 kilograms. |
