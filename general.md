# General ECTN Definitions and Procedures

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-19  
**Approved By:** Alex Russell, Vice President

## Overview

This guide gives general information about ECTN certificates and the application procedure.

An Electronic Cargo Tracking Note (ECTN) is a shipping document. More than 26 African countries require this type of certificate.

The certificate name and the requirements are different for each country.

For requirements specific to each country, refer to the [Country Guides](country-guides/).

## Definitions

### Certificate Names

Different countries use different names for an ECTN certificate.

The terms ECTN, CTN, BESC, and BSC refer to the same type of shipping document.

Some countries use other names for their certificates.

## Shipment Types

The table that follows gives the definitions of common shipment types.

| Type | Definition |
|---|---|
| Container | A shipping container with a length of 20, 40, or 45 feet. The container transports cargo on a vessel. |
| General Cargo / LCL | General cargo includes goods transported as individual packages or pallets. Less than Container Load (LCL) cargo shares a container with other shipments. |
| Bulk | Bulk cargo is transported in large quantities. Examples include grain, clinker, and oil. The measurement unit is usually cubic meters or metric tons. |
| Roro | Roll-on/roll-off (Roro) cargo includes vehicles, tractors, and machinery that move onto a vessel on their wheels. Vehicles transported inside containers are not Roro cargo. |

## How to Determine Which ECTN Certificate is Required for Your Shipment

Use these steps to identify the ECTN certificates required for a shipment:
1. **Identify the consignee country.** The ECTN requirement depends on the country where the consignee is located.
2. **Identify the discharge country.** This is the country where the vessel discharges the cargo.
3. **Open the country guide** for the consignee country.
4. **Examine the shipment routing examples** to identify if the discharge country also requires the ECTN.

## Procedure

### 1. Submit the Documents

Refer to the destination country's guide for the necessary documents.

Send the necessary documents to AfricaCTN by email.

For most countries, you can start the application with a bill of lading.

AfricaCTN will do these tasks:

- Examine the documents
- Prepare the draft ECTN certificate
- Prepare the invoice.

The time necessary to prepare the draft depends on the destination country.

For most countries, AfricaCTN sends the draft certificate and invoice within a few hours.

### 2. Receive the Draft ECTN and Invoice

AfricaCTN will send two PDF documents by email:

- The draft ECTN certificate
- The invoice.

**Draft Certificate**

The draft certificate is the preliminary version of the ECTN.

Use the draft certificate to compare its information with the shipping documents.

You can request corrections to the draft certificate without a penalty.

**Invoice**

The invoice gives the amount that you must pay.

The invoice includes these amounts:

- The ECTN tariff
- The AfricaCTN processing fee.

The payment instructions are at the bottom of the invoice.

### 3. Check and Confirm Details

Compare the draft certificate with the shipping documents.

Make sure that these details are correct:

- The names of the parties on the bill of lading
- The goods description
- The Harmonized System (HS) codes
- The commercial value
- The freight value.

If all details are correct, send your approval to AfricaCTN by email.

If you find an error, send a correction request to AfricaCTN by email.

**Example of approving a draft certificate:**

```text
The draft is approved, please proceed with validation.
```

**Example of requesting a correction to a draft certificate:**

```text
Please correct the spelling of the shipper's name to ABC Shipping.
```

> **Note:** After submission for validation, changes to the certificate are not possible under the general procedure. Country-specific correction rules can differ.

### 4. Pay the Invoice

Use the payment instructions at the bottom of the invoice.

AfricaCTN must receive the payment before submission for validation.

Make sure that these three conditions are complete:

1. AfricaCTN received the payment.
2. You gave approval for the draft certificate.
3. AfricaCTN received all necessary documents.

AfricaCTN will then send the certificate to the relevant authorities for validation.

**Certificate Validation**

Validation is the process in which the authorities examine and approve the ECTN certificate.

The certificate has the status `Draft` before validation.

After the authorities complete validation, the certificate has the status `Validated`.

For more information, refer to [Payment Information](#payment-information).

### 5. Receive the Final Validated Certificate

After the authorities complete validation, AfricaCTN will send the validated ECTN certificate by email.

The certificate is a PDF document.

A physical copy is not necessary.

Use the validated ECTN certificate for customs clearance.

## Certificate Application Stages

The ECTN application has the stages that follow. These stages describe the general process, not the status labels used by each authority.

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | AfricaCTN prepares a draft certificate for the customer to examine. The certificate is not validated. |
| **Draft Approved** | The customer confirms that the information on the draft is correct. Approval does not mean validation is complete. |
| **Submitted for Validation** | AfricaCTN sends the application to the relevant authorities after it receives the payment, draft approval, and necessary documents. |
| **Validated** | The relevant authorities approve the certificate. AfricaCTN sends the validated certificate to the customer by email. |

> **Note:** A draft reference number, customer approval, or payment does not confirm that the certificate is validated.

## Payment Information

### Payment Methods and Instructions

AfricaCTN accepts these payment methods:

- Wire Transfer
- PayCargo
- Zelle
- Credit Card.

Use the payment instructions at the bottom of the invoice.

If you cannot use these payment methods, contact AfricaCTN.

AfricaCTN can arrange a different payment method.

### Payment Receipt Before Submission

AfricaCTN must receive the payment before it can send the ECTN application for validation.

A payment instruction does not confirm that AfricaCTN received the money.

For a bank transfer, the money must be in the AfricaCTN bank account.

Before submission, make sure that these conditions are complete:

1. AfricaCTN received the payment.
2. You gave approval for the draft certificate.
3. AfricaCTN received all necessary documents.

**Payment does not confirm submission or validation.**

After payment, AfricaCTN must send the application to the relevant authorities.

The authorities must then complete the validation.

Make the payment sufficiently early to prevent delays.

Refer to the country guide for the applicable validation deadline.

## Customer Assistance

### Cancellation Requests

Contact an AfricaCTN agent if you want to cancel an ECTN application or certificate.

The agent will examine your request and tell you if cancellation is possible.

The cancellation rules are different for each country and application.

### An Application Already Exists for Your BL

An ECTN application or certificate can already exist for the same bill of lading (BL).

If AfricaCTN identifies an existing application, contact the parties that follow:

- The consignee
- The shipper
- The freight forwarder
- Other parties that arranged the shipment.

Make sure that another party did not already complete the ECTN application.

Identify the party that arranged the existing application or certificate before you continue.
