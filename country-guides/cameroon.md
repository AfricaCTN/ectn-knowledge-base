# Cameroon BESC / ECTN Certificate

## Quick Reference

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Certificate | **Cameroon BESC** |
| Shipments covered | A Cameroon BESC is required for ocean cargo with Cameroon as the final destination. |
| Transit requirement | Cargo transiting Cameroon to a final destination in another country does not require a Cameroon BESC. |
| Validation deadline | The Cameroon BESC must be validated 5 days prior to vessel arrival in the discharge port. |
| Documents | See [Required Documents](#required-documents). |

## Certificate Name and Applicability

The official certificate name for Cameroon is `Bordereau Électronique de Suivi des Cargaisons (BESC)`.

The terms BESC, ECTN, BSC, and CTN refer to the same required certificate.

### Shipments Covered

A Cameroon BESC is required for ocean cargo with Cameroon as the final destination.

- Containerized shipments
- Bulk
- General cargo or LCL
- Roll-on/roll-off (Roro)

>**Note:** Cargo transiting Cameroon to a final destination in another country does **not** require a Cameroon BESC.

### Shipment Routing Examples

The table shows the certificates required for common shipment routes. Final destination means the country of the consignee.

| Port of Discharge (POD) | Final Destination (Consignee Country) | Required Certificate(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Cameroon | Cameroon | **Cameroon BESC** |
| Cameroon | Chad | **Chad ECTN** |
| Cameroon | Central African Republic | **Central African Republic ECTN** |

> **Note:** Each route applies to relevant discharge ports and final delivery locations in the listed countries. The cargo must follow the applicable transit procedure. Check any additional requirements for the discharge country.

## Required Documents

### Bill of Lading (BL)

Reference example: [bill of lading](../examples/shipping-documents/bill-of-lading/maersk.pdf).
- Provide the bill of lading from the shipping line.
- Consignee must be located in Cameroon

### Commercial Invoice

Reference example: [commercial invoice](../examples/shipping-documents/commercial-invoice.pdf).
- Provide a commercial invoice or proforma invoice.

### Freight Invoice

Reference example: [freight invoice](../examples/shipping-documents/freight-invoice.pdf).
- A separate freight invoice is not required if the commercial invoice shows the freight value.
- The freight invoice from the freight forwarder to the shipper is acceptable.

### Consignee TaxID / NIU and PR Number
- `TaxID/NIU`: The consignee's tax identification number. NIU stands for Numéro d'Identification Unique.
- `PR number`: Also called the transaction number.

> **Note:** Both the TaxID and PR number can be found on the consignee's import license. AfricaCTN may already have these details for a registered consignee.

### Conditional Requirements

#### Roro Shipments:
- Provide the `FIMEX` or `Special Import Authorization`
- This requirement does not apply to vehicles shipped inside containers.

### Additional Requirements Before Validation

Not Applicable

## Cameroon BESC Application Process

1. **Submit the Documents:** Send the required documents to AfricaCTN by email. Provide the bill of lading, consignee TaxID, and PR number to start the draft. AfricaCTN will check the documents and prepare the draft BESC and invoice.
2. **Receive the Draft BESC and Invoice:** AfricaCTN will send you two PDF documents by email: the draft BESC certificate and invoice.
3. **Check and Confirm Details:** Check that all details on the draft certificate match your shipping documents. Reply by email to `approve the draft` or `request a correction`.
4. **Pay the Invoice:** Follow the payment instructions at the bottom of the invoice. AfricaCTN must receive your payment before it can submit the certificate for validation. You must also approve the draft and provide all required documents. AfricaCTN will then submit the certificate to the relevant authorities.
5. **Receive the Final Validated Certificate:** The authorities will review and validate the BESC. AfricaCTN will send you the validated certificate by email. You can use this certificate for customs clearance.

**Example of approving a draft certificate:**
```
The draft is approved, please proceed with validation.
```

**Example of requesting a correction to a draft certificate:**
```
Please correct the spelling of the shipper's name to ABC Shipping.
```

> **Note:** Check all details before you approve the draft. Corrections cannot be made after validation.

### BESC Number

The `BESC number` is confirmed only after certificate validation.

The `Demand Number`, issued with the draft, can be used as the BL reference in the meantime.

### Validation Deadline

The Cameroon BESC must be validated `5 days prior to vessel arrival` in the discharge port.

Use the vessel's estimated time of arrival (ETA) shown on the shipping line's tracker to determine the deadline.

The port of loading does not change this deadline.


> **Example:** If the vessel arrival date is `November 20, 2026`, the certificate must be validated before `November 15, 2026`.

#### If the validation deadline is missed:
The consignee must arrange regularization and pay the applicable regularization penalty in Cameroon.

### When to Apply

AfricaCTN recommends applying as soon as your bill of lading is available.

Apply early to allow enough time to:
- Submit the required documents
- Check and approve the draft certificate
- Pay the invoice
- Receive the validated certificate

## Country-Specific Guidance

### Corrections after Validation

Once the certificate is validated, no further corrections can be made.

### Validity of the Cameroon BESC

Each bill of lading should be covered by one BESC number. The same BESC number cannot be used for multiple shipments.

### Responsiblity

The shipper or freight forwarder is responsible for obtaining the validated Cameroon BESC.
