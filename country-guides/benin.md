# Benin BESC / ECTN Certificate

## Quick Reference

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Certificate | **Benin BESC** |
| Shipments covered | A Benin BESC is required for ocean cargo with Benin as the final destination. |
| Transit requirement | Cargo that transits through Benin to another country also requires a Benin BESC. |
| Validation deadline | The Benin BESC must be validated prior to vessel arrival in the discharge port. |
| Documents | See [Required Documents](#required-documents). |

## Certificate Name and Applicability

The official certificate name for Benin is `Bordereau Électronique de Suivi de Cargaison (BESC)`.

The terms BESC, ECTN, BSC, and CTN all refer to the same required certificate.

### Shipments Covered

A Benin BESC is required for ocean cargo with Benin as the final destination.
- Containerized shipments
- Bulk
- General cargo or LCL
- Roll-on/roll-off (Roro)

>**Note:** Cargo that transits through Benin to another country also requires a Benin BESC.

### Shipment Routing Examples

The table shows the certificates required for each route. Final destination means the country of the consignee.

|Port of Discharge (POD)|Final Destination (Consignee Country)|Required Certificate(s)|
|---|---|---|
|Benin|Benin|**Benin BESC**|
|Benin|Togo|**Benin BESC + Togo ECTN**|
|Benin|Burkina Faso|**Benin BESC + Burkina Faso ECTN**|
|Benin|Niger|**Benin BESC + Niger ECTN**|
|Benin|Mali|**Benin BESC + Mali BESC**|
|Benin|Nigeria|**Benin BESC**|

> **Note:** Each route applies to all relevant discharge ports and final delivery locations within the listed countries. The consignee must be located in the final destination country. The table assumes that the cargo follows the stated route and the applicable transit procedure.

### Conditional Requirements

Not applicable.

### Additional Requirements Before Validation

Not Applicable

1. **Submit the Documents:** Send the required documents to AfricaCTN by email. You can start the application with a bill of lading. AfricaCTN will check the documents, and prepare the draft BESC and invoice.
2. **Receive the Draft BESC and Invoice:** AfricaCTN will send you two PDF documents by email: the draft BESC certificate and invoice.
3. **Check and Confirm Details:** Check that all details on the draft certificate match your shipping documents. Reply by email to `approve the draft` or `request a correction`.
4. **Pay the Invoice:** Follow the payment instructions at the bottom of the invoice. AfricaCTN must receive your payment before it can submit the certificate for validation. You must also approve the draft and provide all requried documents. AfricaCTN will then submit the certificate to the relevant authorities.
5. **Receive the Final Validated Certificate:** The authorities will review and validate the BESC. AfricaCTN will send you the validated certificate by email. You can use this certificate for customs clearance.

**Example of approving a draft certificate:**
```
The draft is approved, please proceed with validation.
```

**Example of requesting a correction to a draft certificate:**
```
Please correct the spelling of the shipper's name to ABC Shipping.
```

> **Note:** Check all details before you approve the draft. After validation, corrections require supporting documents.

### BESC Number

Confirm with AfricaCTN when the `BESC number` is available.

Do not assume that a draft reference is a validated certificate number.

### Validation Deadline

The Benin BESC must be validated `prior to vessel arrival` in the discharge port.

Use the vessel's estimated time of arrival (ETA) shown on the shipping line's tracker to determine the deadline.

The port of loading does not change this deadline.


> **Example:** If the vessel arrival date is `November 20, 2026`, the certificate must be validated before `November 20, 2026`.

#### If the validation deadline is missed:
The shipper or consignee must arrange the BESC as soon as possible.

### When to Apply

AfricaCTN recommends applying as soon as your bill of lading is available.

Apply early to allow enough time to:
- Submit the required documents
- Check and approve the draft certificate
- Pay the invoice
- Receive the validated certificate

## Country-Specific Guidance

### Corrections after Validation

You can request corrections after validation.

You must provide supporting documents for the corrections.

### Validity of the Benin BESC

Each bill of lading requires one BESC Certificate.

You cannot use the same BESC certificate for multiple shipments.

### Responsiblity

The shipper or freight forwarder is responsible for obtaining the validated Benin BESC.
