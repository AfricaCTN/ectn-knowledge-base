## Certificate Name and Applicability

The official certificate name for Madagascar is `Bordereau de Suivi des Cargaisons (BSC)`.

Customers commonly refer to it as `BESC`, `ECTN` or `CTN`. Nonetheless, they all refer to the same required document.

### Shipments Covered

Madagascar BSC is required for ocean and air cargo with Madagascar as the final destination.
- Containerized shipments
- Bulk
- General cargo or LCL
- Roll-on/roll-off (Roro)

>**Note:** Cargo in transit through Madagascar **does not require** a Madagascar BSC when the final destination is another country.

## Madagascar BSC Application Process

1. **Submit the Documents:** Send the required documents to AfricaCTN by email. You can start the application with a bill of lading. AfricaCTN will check the documents and prepare the draft BSC and invoice.
2. **Receive the Draft BSC and Invoice:** AfricaCTN will send you two PDF documents by email: the draft BSC certificate and invoice.
3. **Check and Confirm Details:** Check that all details on the draft certificate match your shipping documents. Reply by email to `approve the draft` or `request a correction`.
4. **Pay the Invoice:** Follow the payment instructions at the bottom of the invoice. AfricaCTN must receive your payment before it can submit the certificate for validation. You must also approve the draft and provide all required documents. AfricaCTN will then submit the certificate to the relevant authorities.
5. **Receive the Final Validated Certificate:** The authorities will review and validate the BSC. AfricaCTN will send you the validated certificate by email. You can use this certificate for customs clearance.

**Example of approving a draft certificate:**
```
The draft is approved, please proceed with validation.
```

**Example of requesting a correction to a draft certificate:**
```
Please correct the spelling of the shipper's name to ABC Shipping.
```

> **Note:** Please check the details of the draft certificate very carefully. After it is submitted for validation, changes cannot be guaranteed.

### Validation Deadline

For ocean cargo, the Madagascar BSC must be validated `prior to vessel arrival` in the discharge port.

Use the vessel's estimated time of arrival (ETA) shown on the shipping line's tracker to determine the deadline.

The port of loading does not change this deadline. Confirm the deadline for air shipments with AfricaCTN.


> **Example:** If the vessel arrival date is `November 20, 2026`, the certificate must be validated before `November 20, 2026`.

#### If the validation deadline is missed:
The shipper or consignee must arrange for the BSC certificate as soon as possible.

### When to Apply

AfricaCTN recommends applying as soon as your bill of lading or airway bill is available to allow enough time to submit the documents, review and approve the draft, make payment, and obtain validation.

## Country-Specific Guidance

### Corrections after Validation

Contact AfricaCTN with the supporting documentation to confirm whether a correction is possible.

### Validity of the Madagascar BSC

Each bill of lading should be covered by one BSC certificate. The same BSC certificate cannot be used for multiple shipments.

### Responsiblity

The shipper or freight forwarder is responsible for obtaining the validated Madagascar BSC.
