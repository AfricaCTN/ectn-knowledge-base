# Burkina Faso ECTN Certificate

## Quick Reference

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Certificate | **Burkina Faso ECTN** |
| Shipments covered | A Burkina Faso ECTN is required for ocean cargo with Burkina Faso as the final destination. |
| Transit requirement | Cargo discharging in another country and is in transit to Burkina Faso does require a Burkina Faso BESC. |

| Documents | See [Required Documents](#required-documents). |

## Certificate Name and Applicability

The official certificate name for Burkina Faso is `Electronic Cargo Tracking Note (ECTN)`.

The terms BESC, ECTN, BSC, and CTN refer to the same required certificate.

### Shipments Covered

A Burkina Faso ECTN is required for ocean cargo with Burkina Faso as the final destination.

- Containerized shipments
- Bulk
- General cargo or LCL
- Roll-on/roll-off (Roro)

>**Note:** Cargo discharging in another country and is in transit to Burkina Faso **does require** a Burkina Faso BESC.

### Shipment Routing Examples

The table shows the certificates required for common shipment routes. Final destination means the country of the consignee.

| Port of Discharge (POD) | Final Destination (Consignee Country) | Required Certificate(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Benin | Burkina Faso | **Benin BESC + Burkina Faso ECTN** |
| Togo | Burkina Faso | **Togo ECTN + Burkina Faso ECTN** |
| Ivory Coast | Burkina Faso | **Burkina Faso ECTN** |
| Ghana | Burkina Faso | **Burkina Faso ECTN** |

> **Note:** Each route applies to relevant discharge ports and final delivery locations in the listed countries. The cargo must follow the applicable transit procedure. Check any additional requirements for the discharge country.

- The ECTN number must be added to the bill of lading in order for the certificate to be validated. The ECTN Number will be released along with the draft certificate.

## Burkina Faso ECTN Application Process

1. **Submit the Documents:** Send the required documents to AfricaCTN by email. You can start the application with a bill of lading. AfricaCTN will check the documents and prepare the draft ECTN and invoice.
2. **Receive the Draft ECTN and Invoice:** AfricaCTN will send you two PDF documents by email: the draft ECTN certificate and invoice.
3. **Check and Confirm Details:** Check that all details on the draft certificate match your shipping documents. Reply by email to `approve the draft` or `request a correction`.
4. **Pay the Invoice:** Follow the payment instructions at the bottom of the invoice. AfricaCTN must receive your payment before it can submit the certificate for validation. You must also approve the draft and provide all required documents. AfricaCTN will then submit the certificate to the relevant authorities.
5. **Receive the Final Validated Certificate:** The authorities will review and validate the ECTN. AfricaCTN will send you the validated certificate by email. You can use this certificate for customs clearance.

**Example of approving a draft certificate:**
```
The draft is approved, please proceed with validation.
```

**Example of requesting a correction to a draft certificate:**
```
Please correct the spelling of the shipper's name to ABC Shipping.
```

> **Note:** Please check the details of the draft certificate very carefully. After it is submitted for validation, no further changes will be able to be made.

### Validation Deadline

The Burkina Faso ECTN must be validated `prior to vessel arrival` in the discharge port.

Use the vessel's estimated time of arrival (ETA) shown on the shipping line's tracker to determine the deadline.

The port of loading does not change this deadline.


> **Example:** If the vessel arrival date is `November 20, 2026`, the certificate must be validated before `November 20, 2026`.

#### If the validation deadline is missed:
The shipper or consignee must arrange for the ECTN certificate as soon as possible.

### When to Apply

AfricaCTN recommends applying as soon as your bill of lading is available.

Apply early to allow enough time to:
- Submit the required documents
- Check and approve the draft certificate
- Pay the invoice
- Receive the validated certificate

## Country-Specific Guidance

### Corrections after Validation

Once the certificate is validated, corrections can be made with the supporting documentation.

### Validity of the Burkina Faso BESC

Each bill of lading should be covered by one ECTN certificate. The same ECTN certificate cannot be used for multiple shipments.

### Responsiblity

The shipper or freight forwarder is responsible for obtaining the validated Burkina Faso BESC.
