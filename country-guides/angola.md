# Angola ARCCLA / CNCA Certificate

## Quick Reference

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Certificate | **Angola ARCCLA** |
| Shipments covered | An Angola ARCCLA is required for ocean cargo with Angola as the final destination. |
| Transit requirement | Cargo transiting Angola to a final destination in another country does not require an Angola ARCCLA. |
| Validation deadline | The Angola ARCCLA must be validated prior to vessel arrival in the discharge port. |
| Documents | See [Required Documents](#required-documents). |

## Certificate Name and Applicability

The official certificate name for Angola is `Agência Reguladora de Certificação de Carga e Logística de Angola (ARCCLA)`.

The certificate was formerly known as `Conselho Nacional de Carregadores de Angola (CNCA)`.

The terms BESC, ECTN, BSC, and CTN refer to the same required certificate.

### Shipments Covered

A Angola ARCCLA is required for ocean cargo with Angola as the final destination.

- Containerized shipments
- Bulk
- General cargo or LCL
- Roll-on/roll-off (Roro)

>**Note:** Cargo transiting Angola to a final destination in another country does **not** require an Angola ARCCLA.

### Shipment Routing Examples

The table shows the certificates required for common shipment routes. Final destination means the country of the consignee.

| Port of Discharge (POD) | Final Destination (Consignee Country) | Required Certificate(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Angola | Angola | **Angola ARCCLA** |
| Angola | Democratic Republic of the Congo | **DR Congo FERI** |
| Angola | Republic of Congo | **Republic of Congo ECTN** |

> **Note:** Each route applies to relevant discharge ports and final delivery locations in the listed countries. The cargo must follow the applicable transit procedure. Check any additional requirements for the discharge country.

## Angola ARCCLA Application Process

1. **Submit the Documents:** Send the required documents to AfricaCTN by email. You can start the application with a bill of lading. AfricaCTN will check the documents and prepare the draft ARCCLA and invoice.
2. **Receive the Draft ARCCLA and Invoice:** AfricaCTN will send you two PDF documents by email: the draft ARCCLA certificate and invoice.
3. **Check and Confirm Details:** Check that all details on the draft certificate match your shipping documents. Reply by email to `approve the draft` or `request a correction`.
4. **Pay the Invoice:** Follow the payment instructions at the bottom of the invoice. AfricaCTN must receive your payment before it can submit the certificate for validation. You must also approve the draft and provide all required documents. AfricaCTN will then submit the certificate to the relevant authorities.
5. **Receive the Final Validated Certificate:** The authorities will review and validate the ARCCLA. AfricaCTN will send you the validated certificate by email. You can use this certificate for customs clearance.

**Example of approving a draft certificate:**
```
The draft is approved, please proceed with validation.
```

**Example of requesting a correction to a draft certificate:**
```
Please correct the spelling of the shipper's name to ABC Shipping.
```

> **Note:** Check all details before you approve the draft. After validation, corrections require supporting documents.

### CNCA Number

Confirm with AfricaCTN when the `CNCA number` is available.

Do not assume that a draft reference is a validated certificate number.

### Validation Deadline

The Angola ARCCLA must be validated `prior to vessel arrival` in the discharge port.

Use the vessel's estimated time of arrival (ETA) shown on the shipping line's tracker to determine the deadline.

The port of loading does not change this deadline.


> **Example:** If the vessel arrival date is `November 20, 2026`, the certificate must be validated before `November 20, 2026`.

#### If the validation deadline is missed:
The shipper or consignee must arrange for the ARCCLA certificate as soon as possible with the respective port agent.

### When to Apply

AfricaCTN recommends applying as soon as your bill of lading is available.

Apply early to allow enough time to:
- Submit the required documents
- Check and approve the draft certificate
- Pay the invoice
- Receive the validated certificate

## Country-Specific Guidance

### Corrections after Validation

Once the certificate is validated, corrections can be made with the supporting documentation.

### Validity of the Angola ARCCLA

Each bill of lading should be covered by one ARCCLA certificate. The same ARCCLA certificate cannot be used for multiple shipments.

### Responsiblity

The shipper or freight forwarder is responsible for obtaining the validated Angola ARCCLA.
