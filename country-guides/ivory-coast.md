# Ivory Coast BSC Certificate

## Quick Reference

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Certificate | **Ivory Coast BSC** |
| Shipments covered | Ivory Coast BSC is required for ocean cargo with Ivory Coast as the final destination. |
| Transit requirement | Cargo in transit through Ivory Coast does not require an Ivory Coast BSC when the final destination is another country. |
| Validation deadline | The Ivory Coast BSC must be validated prior to vessel arrival in the discharge port. |
| Documents | See [Required Documents](#required-documents). |

## Certificate Name and Applicability

The official certificate name for Ivory Coast is `Bordereau de Suivi des Cargaisons (BSC)`.

Customers commonly refer to it as `BESC`, `ECTN` or `CTN`. Nonetheless, they all refer to the same required document.

### Shipments Covered

Ivory Coast BSC is required for ocean cargo with Ivory Coast as the final destination.
- Containerized shipments
- Bulk
- General cargo or LCL
- Roll-on/roll-off (Roro)

>**Note:** Cargo in transit through Ivory Coast **does not require** an Ivory Coast BSC when the final destination is another country.

### Shipment Routing Examples

The table shows the certificates required for common shipment routes. Final destination means the country of the consignee.

| Port of Discharge (POD) | Final Destination (Consignee Country) | Required Certificate(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Ivory Coast | Ivory Coast | **Ivory Coast BSC** |
| Ivory Coast | Mali | **Mali BESC** |
| Ivory Coast | Burkina Faso | **Burkina Faso ECTN** |

> **Note:** Each route applies to relevant discharge ports and final delivery locations in the listed countries. The cargo must follow the applicable transit procedure. Check any additional requirements for the discharge country.

## Required Documents

### Bill of Lading (BL)

Reference example: [bill of lading](../examples/shipping-documents/bill-of-lading/maersk.pdf).
- Provide the bill of lading from the shipping line, with the shipped on board date shown.

### Commercial Invoice

Reference example: [commercial invoice](../examples/shipping-documents/commercial-invoice.pdf).
- The commercial invoice must show the goods value, currency and Incoterm.

### Freight Invoice

Reference example: [freight invoice](../examples/shipping-documents/freight-invoice.pdf).
- A separate freight invoice is not required if the commercial invoice shows the freight value.

### Export Declaration / Customs Declaration

Reference example: [export declaration](../examples/shipping-documents/export-declaration.pdf).
- The export declaration's value must match the commercial invoice and its weight must match the bill of lading

### Packing List

Reference example: [packing list](../examples/shipping-documents/packing-list.pdf).
- The packing details and weight must match the commercial invoice and bill of lading.

### FDI (Import Licence)
- The FDI must match the consignee and its number must match the certificate of insurance.

### Certificate of Origin

Reference example: [certificate of origin](../examples/shipping-documents/certificate-of-origin.pdf).
- A certificate of origin is required.

### Certificate of Insurance

Reference example: [insurance certificate](../examples/shipping-documents/certificate-of-insurance.pdf).
- A certificate of insurance is required.

### Conditional Requirements

Not Applicable

### Additional Requirements Before Validation
- The consignee must be registered with on the [Ivory Coast System](https://bsci.sgs.com/Pages/Public/Default.aspx).
- A final dated bill of lading is required.

## Ivory Coast BSC Application Process

1. **Submit the Documents:** Send the required documents to AfricaCTN by email. Provide the bill of lading to start the draft. AfricaCTN will check the documents and prepare the draft BSC and invoice.
2. **Receive the Draft BSC and Invoice:** AfricaCTN will send you two PDF documents by email: the draft BSC certificate and invoice.
3. **Check and Confirm Details:** Check that all details on the draft certificate match your shipping documents. Reply by email to `approve the draft` or `request a correction`.
4. **Pay the Invoice:** Follow the payment instructions at the bottom of the invoice. AfricaCTN must receive your payment before it can submit the certificate for validation. You must also approve the draft and provide all required documents. AfricaCTN will then submit the certificate to the relevant authorities.
5. **Receive the Final Validated Certificate:** The authorities will review and validate the BSC. AfricaCTN will send you the validated certificate by email. You can use this certificate for customs clearance.

**Example of approving a draft certificate:**
```
The draft is approved, please proceed with validation.
```

**Example of requesting a correction to a draft certificate:**
```
Please correct the spelling of the shipper's name to ABC Shipping.
```

> **Note:** Please check the details of the draft certificate very carefully. After it is submitted for validation, changes cannot be guaranteed.

### Validation Deadline

The Ivory Coast BSC must be validated `prior to vessel arrival` in the discharge port.

Use the vessel's estimated time of arrival (ETA) shown on the shipping line's tracker to determine the deadline.

The port of loading does not change this deadline.


> **Example:** If the vessel arrival date is `November 20, 2026`, the certificate must be validated before `November 20, 2026`.

#### If the validation deadline is missed:
The shipper or consignee must arrange for the BSC certificate as soon as possible.

### When to Apply

AfricaCTN recommends applying as soon as your bill of lading is available.

Apply early to allow enough time to:
- Submit the required documents
- Check and approve the draft certificate
- Pay the invoice
- Receive the validated certificate

## Country-Specific Guidance

### Corrections after Validation

Contact AfricaCTN with the supporting documentation to confirm whether a correction is possible.

### Validity of the Ivory Coast BSC

Each bill of lading should be covered by one BSC certificate. The same BSC certificate cannot be used for multiple shipments.

### Responsiblity

The shipper or freight forwarder is responsible for obtaining the validated Ivory Coast BSC.
